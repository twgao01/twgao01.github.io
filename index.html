About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Prof. Zhun Deng. Before joining UNC, I received my bachelor’s degree from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Peking University.

My research focuses on the theoretical foundations of efficient human–AI collaboration and multi-agent systems.
